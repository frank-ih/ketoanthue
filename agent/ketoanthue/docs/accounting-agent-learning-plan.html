Accounting Agent Learning Plan

How the agent acquires, validates, and operationalizes accounting & tax knowledge — starting with Vietnam. Source taxonomy, ingestion pipeline, knowledge graph analysis, user stories, and trust architecture.

📚
Source Taxonomy & Trust Hierarchy

Not all knowledge is equal. The agent must learn from a strict hierarchy: law first, then official guidance, then Big-4 analysis, then community — never the reverse. Every piece of knowledge carries a trust tier.

🎯 Core Principle: Selective & Tiered Ingestion

The agent does NOT crawl the internet randomly. It follows a 4-tier trust hierarchy. Higher tiers override lower tiers when they conflict. The agent always cites its source tier when giving advice, and refuses to act on Tier 4 content alone for anything involving journal entries, tax calculations, or compliance.

Tier 1 Primary Law

Authoritative & immutable. The actual text of laws, decrees, and circulars as published by the government. The agent treats this as ground truth. No interpretation — just the law.

Tier 2 Official Guidance

Interpretive authority. Official guidance documents (công văn), ministry Q&A, GDT instructions, and official FAQs from tax authorities. These clarify how to apply Tier 1 laws.

Tier 3 Expert Analysis

Professional interpretation. Big-4 publications (PwC, KPMG, EY, Deloitte), law firms, licensed accounting firms. High quality but represents opinion, not authority.

Tier 4 Community / Education

Practical but unverified. Accounting blogs, training sites, forums, how-to articles. Useful for understanding common practices but never authoritative. Cross-check against Tier 1-2 before use.

⚠️ Critical Filter Rules

1. Regulation citations required: Any accounting guidance must reference a specific law, circular, or decree number. Content without legal references is flagged as "unverified practice" and cannot be used for journal entries or tax calculations.

2. Date-aware override: Circular 99/2025 replaces Circular 200/2014 from Jan 1, 2026. The agent must track effective dates and automatically deprecate superseded content. Same for Decree 70/2025 replacing Decree 123/2020 on e-invoicing.

3. No Tier-4-only decisions: If the only supporting evidence for an accounting treatment comes from Tier 4 sources, the agent MUST escalate to a human accountant rather than proceed.

4. Vietnamese language priority: Original Vietnamese legal text is authoritative. English translations are for reference only. The agent must store and reason from Vietnamese originals.

Phase 1: Discovery & Crawl

  • Scheduled crawlers per source: thuvienphapluat.vn checked every 6 hours for new circulars/decrees; gdt.gov.vn daily; Big-4 sites weekly
  • Change detection: Hash-based for legal docs (any change triggers re-ingestion); date-based for articles
  • Priority queue: Tier 1 changes jump to front of ingestion queue and trigger downstream re-validation
  • Format handling: HTML article → markdown; PDF circular → OCR + structured extraction; DOCX appendices (CoA tables) → JSON

Phase 2: Parse & Structure

  • Legal document parser: Splits circulars into Điều (Article) → Khoản (Clause) → Điểm (Point) hierarchy. Each unit gets a unique ID: TT99-2025.Đ31.K2.Đ.a
  • Metadata extraction: Document number, issuing authority, effective date, superseded document, amendment chain
  • Chart of Accounts parser: Extract official CoA from Circular 99 appendix into structured {code, name, level, parent, debit_nature, credit_nature}
  • Tax rate extractor: Parse VAT rates, CIT rates, PIT brackets into versioned lookup tables with effective date ranges

Phase 3: Validate & Filter

  • Legal reference check: Every knowledge chunk must cite at least one Circular/Decree/Law number to pass. Chunks without citations are quarantined.
  • Cross-tier validation: Tier 4 content (e.g., MISA blog example showing Dr 641/Cr 111) is validated against Tier 1 account definitions in Circular 99 CoA
  • Contradiction detection: If Tier 3 analysis contradicts Tier 1 text, flag for human review. The system defaults to Tier 1.
  • Effective date filter: Automatically tag content as CURRENT, TRANSITIONAL, or SUPERSEDED based on circular effective dates
  • Staleness detection: Content referencing Circular 200 after Jan 1 2026 is auto-flagged as potentially outdated

Phase 4: Chunk & Embed

  • Semantic chunking: Legal articles stay as atomic units (don't split mid-article). Practical guides chunked by accounting topic/GL account.
  • Rich metadata per chunk: trust_tier, source_url, document_number, effective_date, gl_accounts[], tax_types[], industry_codes[]
  • Multi-lingual embedding: Jina v5 (jina-embeddings-v5-text-small) — 1024-dim vectors with retrieval.passage / retrieval.query task types. Native Vietnamese + English support. Falls back to deterministic hash-based stubs when API key is unavailable.
  • Qdrant collection per country: accounting_vn, future: accounting_sg, accounting_th

Does the Accounting Agent Need a Knowledge Graph?

Analyzing whether Apache AGE (the graph extension already in the AgentClan stack) adds meaningful value for accounting knowledge specifically.

🎯 Verdict: YES — but scoped, not everything.

Accounting is inherently relational — accounts have parent-child hierarchies, transactions link vendors to invoices to GL codes to tax types to reporting lines. A flat vector store struggles with multi-hop queries like "which expense accounts under COGS (TK 632) are affected by the new Circular 99 depreciation rules?" The graph excels at exactly these structural relationships. However, NOT everything needs to go in the graph. Only structural and relational data; procedural knowledge stays in the vector store.

What belongs IN the Knowledge Graph

  • Chart of Accounts hierarchy: Account 6 → 62 → 621 → 6211 parent-child, debit/credit nature, which Circular defines it
  • Regulation dependency graph: Circular 99 → supersedes → Circular 200; Decree 70 → amends → Decree 123; Law 48/2024 → governs → VAT rates
  • Account → Tax type mapping: TK 1331 → Deductible VAT; TK 3331 → Output VAT; TK 3334 → CIT payable
  • Transaction pattern templates: "Purchase on credit" → Dr 152 + Dr 1331, Cr 331 (linked as pattern nodes)
  • Vendor/Customer → Invoice → Journal Entry → GL Account links (per-tenant operational data)
  • Effective date relationships: "TK 158 → defined_by → Circular 99, Article X → effective_from → 2026-01-01"

What stays OUT (Vector Store only)

  • Full text of laws and circulars — too long, no relational structure, better served by semantic search
  • Practical how-to guides — MISA articles, Big-4 explainers, training content → pure retrieval task
  • Q&A pairs from công văn — factoid retrieval, not multi-hop reasoning
  • Journal entry worked examples — few-shot examples for the LLM, not graph traversal
  • Tax rate tables — simple lookup table, overkill for graph

📐 Proposed Graph Schema (Apache AGE)

Lean schema — only nodes and edges that enable multi-hop queries the vector store can't answer alone.

// Node types
(:Account {code, name, level, parent_code, nature, circular_ref, effective_from})
(:Regulation {number, title, issuing_authority, effective_date, superseded_by, status})
(:TaxType {code, name, current_rate, rate_history[]})
(:TransactionPattern {name, description, debit_accounts[], credit_accounts[]})
(:Industry {code, name})
(:FinancialStatement {template_code, name, line_items[]})

// Edge types
(:Account)-[:CHILD_OF]->(:Account)
(:Account)-[:DEFINED_BY]->(:Regulation)
(:Account)-[:MAPS_TO_TAX]->(:TaxType)
(:Account)-[:APPEARS_IN]->(:FinancialStatement)
(:Regulation)-[:SUPERSEDES]->(:Regulation)
(:Regulation)-[:AMENDS]->(:Regulation)
(:TransactionPattern)-[:USES_DEBIT]->(:Account)
(:TransactionPattern)-[:USES_CREDIT]->(:Account)
(:TransactionPattern)-[:APPLICABLE_TO]->(:Industry)
(:TaxType)-[:GOVERNED_BY]->(:Regulation)

🔍 Example Multi-Hop Queries Only the Graph Can Answer Efficiently

  • "Which accounts were added or changed by Circular 99 compared to Circular 200?" → Traverse DEFINED_BY edges, compare
  • "What GL accounts should I use to record a purchase of fixed assets on credit with VAT?" → TransactionPattern → USES_DEBIT/CREDIT → Account
  • "Show me all expense accounts that roll up to line item 'Cost of Goods Sold' on the P&L" → Account ← CHILD_OF chain ← APPEARS_IN → B02-DN
  • "Which regulations govern account TK 3334 (CIT payable) and are currently in effect?" → Account → MAPS_TO_TAX → TaxType → GOVERNED_BY → Regulation (status=CURRENT)
  • "If Circular 99 changes the definition of TK 158, which transaction patterns are affected?" → Regulation → DEFINED_BY ← Account ← USES_DEBIT/CREDIT ← TransactionPattern

User Stories — Verifying Knowledge Demands

Real scenarios that test whether the agent's knowledge is complete, accurate, and actionable. Each story maps to specific knowledge requirements.

Daily — Invoice Processing

US-01: Record a purchase invoice to the general ledger

User action: Uploads a PDF invoice from vendor "Công ty TNHH ABC" for office supplies. Invoice shows: Total 11,000,000 VND (goods: 10,000,000 VND + VAT 10%: 1,000,000 VND). PO #2024-0156 exists in the system.

User says: "Hạch toán hóa đơn này giúp tôi" (Record this invoice for me)

Agent must know: OCR the invoice fields → validate against PO → classify as office expense (TK 6422 or TK 6421 depending on company's CoA structure) → create journal entry: Dr 6422 (Management expense) 10,000,000 / Dr 1331 (Deductible input VAT) 1,000,000 / Cr 331 (Trade payables - ABC) 11,000,000. Must confirm the VAT is currently 8% or 10% (check if office supplies qualify for the 2% reduction under Resolution 204/2025). Must verify e-invoice validity format per Decree 70/2025.

Daily — Revenue Recognition

US-02: Record a sales transaction with exported goods

User action: Provides a sales contract for export of garments to a US buyer. FOB value: 50,000 USD. Exchange rate at transaction date: 25,450 VND/USD. VAT rate for exports: 0%.

User says: "Record this export sale. The goods shipped yesterday."

Agent must know: Export sales are VAT 0% (not VAT-exempt — important distinction for input VAT deduction). Revenue recognized when risk/reward transfers (VAS, not IFRS control model). Journal entry: Dr 131 (Trade receivable) 1,272,500,000 / Cr 5111 (Revenue - goods) 1,272,500,000. Must use the actual exchange rate per Circular 99 Article 31. Must know that export requires customs declaration as supporting document.

Monthly — Period Close

US-03: Calculate monthly depreciation for all fixed assets

User action: End of month. Company has 15 fixed assets with varying useful lives (3-10 years).

User says: "Run monthly depreciation calculation and create the journal entries."

Agent must know: Depreciation method (straight-line is standard VAS). Useful life limits per Circular 45/2013 (e.g., computers: 3-5 years, vehicles: 6-10 years, buildings: 25-50 years). Journal entry per asset: Dr 6274 (Factory depreciation) or Dr 6424 (Admin depreciation) / Cr 214 (Accumulated depreciation). Must respect minimum capitalization threshold (currently 30M VND and useful life >1 year per Circular 45). Must flag any fully depreciated assets still in use.

Quarterly — Tax Filing

US-04: Prepare quarterly VAT declaration

User action: End of Q1. Company uses credit-invoice method (khấu trừ).

User says: "Prepare the VAT return for Q1. Tell me if we owe or get a refund."

Agent must know: Aggregate all TK 33311 (Output VAT) and TK 1331 (Input VAT) for Q1. Apply the 8% reduced rate where applicable per Resolution 204/2025. Calculate: Output VAT - Deductible Input VAT = VAT payable or refundable. If refundable and >300M VND and meets criteria, advise on refund application. Generate data in HTKK-compatible format. Must know VAT filing deadline: 30th of month following quarter end.

Compliance — Regulation Change

US-05: "What changed in Circular 99 vs. Circular 200?"

User says: "We're transitioning to Circular 99 for FY2026. What are the key changes I need to prepare for? Specifically for our Chart of Accounts and financial statement templates."

Agent must know (via Knowledge Graph): Traverse the Regulation graph to identify: Circular 99 now allows flexible CoA (vs. rigid prescribed CoA in TT200). New accounts added (e.g., biological assets). Enterprises must now issue Internal Governance Accounting Policy (IGAP). Electronic vouchers have same legal weight as paper. Financial statement templates updated. The agent must produce a side-by-side comparison and a migration checklist.

Complex — Contract Analysis

US-06: Analyze a service contract for correct accounting treatment

User action: Uploads a 12-month IT service contract worth 240M VND (20M/month). Payment: 50% upfront, 50% after 6 months. Service starts March 1, 2026.

User says: "How do I account for this contract? We received the first 120M today."

Agent must know: The 120M VND received upfront is NOT revenue yet — it's a liability (TK 3387 - Unearned revenue). Revenue recognized monthly at 20M VND as service is delivered. Journal entries: On receipt: Dr 112 (Bank) 120M / Cr 3387 (Unearned revenue) 120M. Monthly: Dr 3387 20M / Cr 5113 (Service revenue) 20M. Must handle VAT timing: e-invoice must be issued per payment milestone per Decree 70/2025 rules. Must know VAS revenue recognition criteria.

Three Pillars of Trust & Accuracy

Pillar 1: Knowledge Integrity

  • Tiered trust system — law overrides opinion, always
  • Mandatory citations — every output references regulation numbers
  • Effective date tracking — superseded rules auto-deprecated
  • Cross-validation — Tier 4 content verified against Tier 1 before operational use
  • Vietnamese original priority — English translations are secondary

Pillar 2: Computational Accuracy

  • Double-entry validation — every journal entry checked: Dr = Cr
  • Deterministic tax calculations — code-based, not LLM-generated math
  • CoA constraint checking — GL codes validated against graph schema
  • Reconciliation loops — auto-check totals before period close
  • Decimal precision — VND has no subunit, enforce integer arithmetic

Pillar 3: Operational Safety

  • Confidence-based escalation — < 0.85 confidence → human review
  • Approval gates — payroll, tax filings, period close require human sign-off
  • Immutable audit trail — every action logged with user, timestamp, source, reasoning
  • Shadow testing — regulation changes tested on historical data before live deployment
  • Role-based access — accountant vs. manager vs. auditor see different capabilities
